## About Source V1
With nearly four decades in fencing and more than 30 years of international coaching experience, Coach Yousef Alaghehband has coached athletes and developed fencing programs across five continents — from grassroots and school fencing to national-team and international competition levels.

Throughout his career, his work has extended beyond coaching individual athletes. He has helped develop champions, establish and grow fencing programs, educate coaches, and build international collaborations between schools, fencing clubs, academies, and sports organizations.

His experience spans the full athlete-development pathway, from kindergarten and school-based programs to high-performance and international-level fencing, giving him a unique understanding of how successful fencing programs can be introduced, developed, and sustained.
